VIA: a 4,000 km unsupported ultracycling race across Europe organised by ultracyclists, for ultracyclists.

## The story so far

- **Chapter I (2024)** retraced Hannibal's bold crossing of the Alps, with 38 elephants, ending in southern Italy.
- **Chapter II (2025)** followed Germanicus' quest to recover Rome's lost Eagles, from the Adriatic to the North Sea, Italy to the Netherlands.
- **Chapter III (2026)** pushes beyond Rome's frontier into the legendary North, to the most breathtakingly beautiful places in the world.
