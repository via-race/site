We're thrilled to announce **two exciting updates to our website**: fresh **info on Chapter II** and, even better, **our brand-new blog**! This space is where we’ll share stories, interviews, news, curiosities, and much more—all capturing the essence of VIA and the spirit of our community.

As we launch, we’re taking a moment to celebrate the core beliefs that shape VIA: community, adventure, competition, and, what we value the most, diversity and inclusion. VIA is about embracing what makes each of us unique, while challenging ourselves together on the road to greatness.

“We’re all different!” - “No, I’m not!”**

This week, we’re spotlighting diversity in our VIA community, reaching out to all who may feel they bring something unique to the race. Whether it’s you, a friend, or someone you know, we’re calling out to underrepresented groups: **women**, **paracyclists**, **people of color**, **neurodivergent individuals**, or riders **on “unconventional” bikes**—like Allan Shaw’s cargo bike or David Sherrington’s Brompton. 

**VIA is about inclusion and celebrating every rider’s distinctiveness.**

To kick things off, we’ve released **<u>[a short video](https://youtu.be/evdD-Wcoej0)</u>** showcasing the inspiring Women of VIA Chapter I on Instagram and YouTube. And due to popular demand, ultracyclist and co-founder Ingeborg, alongside other incredible women of Chapter I, will host a special women-only webinar on Tuesday, November 12th at 19:00 UK time.

Know someone who should join? Please help us spread the word. Let’s bring more women into the VIA community!

<u>**Sign up here: [Google Form Link](https://forms.gle/1Ry6gj7QwZ64CJxz8)**</u>

Stay tuned as we continue to explore new stories, inspire each other, and build a stronger, more inclusive VIA community. We’re just getting started, and we’re so glad to have you with us on this journey.
